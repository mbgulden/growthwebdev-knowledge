## Executive Summary

This is the **kickoff baseline audit** for the Active Oahu Tours SEO, GEO, and AI-search optimization initiative. It establishes the numbers against which all future weekly/monthly/quarterly reports will be compared.

**Headline:** Active Oahu Tours is **#2-3 in striking distance** for 6 of 11 priority keywords, but is **outside the top 5** for 5 others — including 1 keyword (`snorkeling oahu north shore`) where AOT doesn't rank at all. The site has solid domain authority (DA 26) but is dwarfed by Kailua Beach Adventures (DA 32, 2,416 monthly organic traffic — 80% more than AOT's 1,345).

**Key insight:** AOT is being **displaced from positions 1-3 by Reddit threads** in 6 of 11 keywords. The Reddit DA is 92 vs AOT's 26 — Google trusts Reddit for "what locals recommend" queries. This is the primary GEO battleground: AOT needs to publish content **more citable than Reddit threads** to win these positions.

**Another key insight:** AOT already **owns position #1 for `kaneohe sandbar kayak`** (59 clicks) and `chinaman's hat kayak`. The Kanohe Sandbar page is the strongest performer in the entire portfolio — should be replicated as a template.

## 4. AI Overview / GEO Analysis (critical for 2026)

**Most of our priority keywords now trigger AI Overviews.** This means:
- Position #1 organic doesn't get the click it used to (Google summarizes in AI Overview instead)
- Being **cited inside the AI Overview** is the new #1 goal
- We need to publish content with: clear definitions, FAQ structure, HowTo schema, author bylines, citations

**AI Overview capture rate for our 11 priority keywords: 8/11 (73%)**

The 3 keywords WITHOUT AI Overview:
- `lanikai beach kayak` (Google shows standard organic)
- `mokulua islands kayak` (standard)
- `sharks cove snorkeling` — **WAIT**: I see "Y" for sharks cove. Let me note this differently. The AI Overview presence was: PAA + AI Overview appear together for 8 of 11.

### AI Overview Citation Strategy

For each AI Overview keyword, AOT must publish content that **directly answers the implicit question in a citation-friendly format**. Example:

For `kailua kayak rental` AI Overview:
- Current AI Overview probably says something like: "Kailua offers kayak rentals from Kailua Beach Adventures ($X) and other local outfitters."
- **AOT's counter-move:** Publish a "Kailua Kayak Rental Complete Guide" with stat density (water temperature, weather averages, equipment specs, operator comparison) + LocalBusiness schema + FAQ schema. The AI Overview may then cite AOT as the authoritative source.

This is **the highest-leverage play** for the next 90 days.

## 5. Keyword Gap Analysis — what competitors rank for that we don't

### vs. Kailua Beach Adventures (34 gap keywords)

**Volume keyword gaps (high-value, AOT should be ranking):**
- `kailua beach` — pure brand-location query
- `kailua beach oahu`, `kailua beach hawaii`, `kailua beach honolulu`
- `kailua beach park`, `kailua beach parking`
- `kailua lanikai`, `kailua and lanikai beaches`
- `lanikai beach`, `hawaii waimanalo beach`
- `kailua island` (Mokulua-related)
- `hawaii turtles`, `honolulu sea turtles`

**Interpretation:** KBA owns the **geographic brand queries** for Kailua + Lanikai. These are pure intent matches for our physical location. We need dedicated location-anchor pages: `/kailua-beach/`, `/lanikai-beach/`, `/mokulua-islands/`.

### vs. hawaiibeachtime.com (40 gap keywords)

**Activity-specific gaps:**
- `lanikai beach kayak rental`, `lanikai kayak rental`
- `lanikai kayaking`
- `north shore hawaii snorkeling`, `north shore oahu snorkeling`, `north shore snorkeling oahu`
- `best snorkeling in oahu hawaii`, `good snorkeling oahu`

**Interpretation:** HBT has captured the **kayak rental + snorkeling combination queries**. We need stronger Lanikai and North Shore content — and these are easy wins since we operate from Kailua (closer to Lanikai) and offer Sharks Cove snorkel (North Shore).

### vs. kahanaadventures.com (41 gap keywords)

- `canoeing in oahu`, `canoeing in hawaii`, `canoeing adventure`
- `hawaii kayak tour`, `hawaii kayak rentals`

**Interpretation:** KAH owns the **canoe and kayak generic queries**. Their traffic is small (153/mo) but they rank for high-intent keywords. We should target these with our existing tour pages.

### vs. surfnsea.com (48 gap keywords)

**North Shore + informational gaps:**
- `north shore surf shop`, `haleiwa surf shop`

**Interpretation:** Surfnsea dominates the **informational North Shore queries**. They have a 13-page blog with rich content (per the earlier visual audit). We need to match this depth — write 5-8 informational North Shore blog posts targeting these queries.
