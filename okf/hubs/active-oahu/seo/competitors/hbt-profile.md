### Key observation

**HBT's #1 page is "Oahu's North Shore Snorkeling" with 983 visits/mo.** AOT's Sharks Cove page gets 589 clicks/mo (real GSC). **HBT is winning North Shore snorkel informational queries.**

## Counter-strategy

HBT is less threatening than KBA because:
- Lower DA (24 vs AOT 26)
- Delivery model (different from AOT's storefront pickup)
- Smaller content library
- Less topical authority

### Tactical responses

1. **"Oahu's North Shore Snorkeling" page** — AOT has Sharks Cove page. Refresh with:
   - More wildlife data (turtle species, fish species)
   - Safety section (when NOT to snorkel)
   - Gear comparison vs HBT's delivery
   - Specific Sharks Cove vs Waimea vs Three Tables comparison

2. **"Lanikai Beach Kayak Rental"** — AOT has multiple Kailua kayak pages. Consolidate + improve.

3. **Waimea Bay content** — AOT doesn't have a Waimea Bay page. NEW opportunity.

## Strategic recommendation

**Watch HBT but don't over-invest.** AOT already has Sharks Cove dominance. Focus on KBA first.
