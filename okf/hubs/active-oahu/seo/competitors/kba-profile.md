### Key observation

**KBA's #1 page is "Discover Lanikai Beach" with 111,934 visits/mo** — that's 3000x more than AOT's Lanikai guide (37 visits/mo). This is AOT's biggest content gap.

KBA's content strategy:
- 1 mega-pillar per location (Lanikai, Kailua Beach Park, Waimanalo)
- 1 guide per activity type (kayaking, SUP, biking, snorkeling)
- Strong internal linking between guide + rental/tour pages
- Each guide is 2,000-5,000 words with stats, photos, FAQs

## Content gaps AOT must close

1. **/guides/lanikai-beach-guide/** — KBA gets 111,934 visits/mo on their Lanikai guide. **AOT's guide gets 37 visits/mo.** Gap = **111,897 visits/mo potential**.

2. **/guides/kailua-beach-park-guide/** — KBA gets 25,471 visits/mo. AOT equivalent gets ~10 clicks/mo. Gap = **~25,461 visits/mo potential**.

3. **/guides/waimanalo-beach-guide/** — KBA gets 1,372 visits/mo on Waimanalo guide. AOT equivalent: tier-1 brief BRIEF-001 (not yet written).

4. **Snorkel guides** — KBA has "Sea Turtles on Oahu" guide (732 visits/mo). AOT has nothing comparable.

5. **Mokulua/Mokes guides** — KBA has "Complete Guide to Kayaking to the Mokes" (979 visits/mo). AOT equivalent exists but underperforms.

## Counter-strategy

### What AOT can do better than KBA

1. **Real storefront authority** — AOT has a physical storefront at 134B Hamakua Dr. Use this in content ("Stop by our Kailua shop before launching").
2. **Photo library depth** — 14,490 photos vs KBA's likely ~500. Use real customer photos, not stock.
3. **Operator voice** — First-person content from Kimo + team. KBA sounds corporate.
4. **Stat density** — Real water temps, weather averages, tide data. KBA's content is generic.
5. **E-E-A-T signals** — Author bylines, founder bio, real customer reviews with photos.

### Tactical responses to KBA's top 5 pages

1. **Lanikai guide** (111K visits/mo target) — Write 4,000-word pillar. Include:
   - Real parking info (with photos)
   - Lanikai vs Kailua comparison
   - Mokulua Islands kayak launch from Lanikai
   - Snorkeling (KBA's weakness here)
   - Weather + tide data
   - Stat density

2. **Kailua Beach Park guide** (25K visits/mo target) — Mirror their structure but add:
   - 134B Hamakua Dr. storefront visit callout
   - Real parking situation photos
   - When to go (winter vs summer, weekday vs weekend)
   - Equipment rental bundled with guide

3. **Waimanalo guide** (1,372 visits/mo target) — BRIEF-001 content. Make it statistically dense.

4. **Sea turtles guide** (732 visits/mo target) — NEW PAGE for AOT. Position as "ethical wildlife viewing" — KBA doesn't emphasize this.

5. **Mokes kayaking guide** (979 visits/mo target) — Refresh AOT's existing page with more detail, better photos, FAQ schema.

## Strategic recommendation

**Spend Month 1-2 closing the Lanikai + Kailua Beach Park + Waimanalo content gaps.** If AOT can capture even 10% of KBA's traffic on these pages, that's **+13,000 visits/mo potential**.
