# LoveOahu — Detailed Competitor Profile

**Domain:** loveoahu.org
**Threat level:** 🟡 Low-Medium — informational blog, NOT a competitor for bookings

## Domain metrics

| Metric | LoveOahu | AOT | Gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Domain Authority | **22** | 26 | -4 |
| Monthly organic traffic | **4,822** | ~452 (real GSC) | +4,370/mo |
| Backlinks | **10,102** | 1,354 | -8,748 |

**LoveOahu has 4,822 monthly organic traffic — 10x AOT.** But DA 22 is LOWER than AOT 26.

## Content strategy

LoveOahu publishes **comprehensive Oahu travel guides** — "best beaches", "things to do", "where to stay", etc. Heavy on informational intent.

## Counter-strategy

### What AOT can do better

1. **Transactional intent** — LoveOahu is purely informational. AOT can convert their readers into bookers.
2. **Operator voice** — LoveOahu writes ABOUT Oahu. AOT operates IN Oahu.
3. **E-E-A-T** — Author bylines, real reviews, real photos.

### Tactical responses

1. **"Best Oahu Beaches"** — AOT should publish pillar at /guides/best-beaches-windward-oahu/ (BRIEF-009)
2. **"Things to Do in Oahu"** — AOT's tour pages should rank for these queries
3. **Link bridge** — Add a section "Book a tour with a local operator" on broad informational pages

## Strategic recommendation

**LoveOahu is a referral source, not a competitor.** When their readers want to BOOK something, AOT should be the obvious choice. Approach LoveOahu for backlinks/partnerships.
