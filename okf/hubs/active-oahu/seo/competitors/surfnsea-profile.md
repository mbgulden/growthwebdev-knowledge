### Key observation

**Surfnsea's #2 page is "Where to See Turtles on Oahu" with 962 visits/mo.** AOT has nothing on this. **Major gap.**

Surfnsea's content angles:
- Marine life (turtles, sharks, reefs) — AOT can match
- Surfing history + culture — NOT AOT's lane
- Beach guides for North Shore beaches (Haleiwa, Waimea) — AOT can expand
- Product pages (HURLEY tees, Haleiwa Ukuleles) — retail, not service

## Counter-strategy

### What AOT can compete on

1. **"Where to See Turtles on Oahu"** — NEW PAGE opportunity. AOT operates Sharks Cove (where turtles often appear). Write this from operator perspective with photos.
2. **"Sharks Cove Guide"** — Surfnsea has a brief one (96 visits/mo). AOT can do better with real operator voice + photos.
3. **"Complete Guide to Oahu's North Shore Beaches"** — 44 visits/mo for Surfnsea. Write AOT's own version with Kimo's first-hand knowledge.

### What to avoid competing on

- Surfing history (Surfnsea's lane)
- Surfing tips / techniques (different audience)
- Surf shop retail (different business model)
- Haleiwa culture (their location)

## Strategic recommendation

**Use Surfnsea's marine life content as inspiration, but own the operator perspective.** Surfnsea writes ABOUT Oahu. AOT operates IN Oahu — that's the differentiator.
