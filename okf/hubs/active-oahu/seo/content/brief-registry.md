## How to use this registry

For each brief, Ella gets:
- **Target URL** — the page to write or refresh
- **Cluster + keywords** — which keywords to target (master-inventory.md has full context)
- **Questions to answer** — exact questions from question-inventory.md (drives H2/H3 structure + FAQ schema)
- **Target word count** — for content quality scoring
- **Image requirements** — which photos from Synology to use
- **Internal links** — cross-cluster linking per GRO-795 hub-and-spoke
- **Schema requirements** — JSON-LD to add
- **AEO block** — 40-80 word direct answer at top of page

Kai-CSS deploys. Kai verifies. Michael approves.
