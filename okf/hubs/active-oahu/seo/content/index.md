## Two parallel work streams

### 1. NEW content (brief-registry.md)

Building new pages from scratch. 20 briefs defined covering 43,200 words.

### 2. REFRESH existing content (per-page-refresh-briefs.md)

Refreshing existing pages to push rankings up. 20 briefs covering top GSC-traffic pages.

**Refresh work has higher immediate ROI** because existing pages already have authority + backlinks + indexation. New pages take months to gain traction.

## Recommendation

**Start with REFRESH-001 through REFRESH-005** in Week 1-2. These are existing pages with high traffic potential.

**Add NEW pages** (Lanikai pillar, Kailua Beach Park pillar, Waimanalo, Sea Turtles) in Week 3-4 once refreshes show movement.
