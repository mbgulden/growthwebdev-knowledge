## Key Concepts

- **Information Gain (IG):** Mathematical framework measuring net new, empirically verifiable data a document contributes to the aggregate corpus
- **Stat Density:** Pages with 5+ specific statistical data points per 1,000 words cited 3x more often by generative models
- **Zero-Click Ecosystem:** Answer engines resolve intent directly in SERP — brands must optimize for extraction, not just clicks
- **AEO Quick Answer Blocks:** 50-75 word precision-engineered blocks for LLM extraction, placed immediately below H1/H2
- **Consensus Baseline:** Standard claims all top 10 competitors publish — Active Oahu must explicitly avoid to ensure high IG

## Competitive Landscape

- **Primary competitors:** Kailua Beach Adventures, Blue Planet Surf
- **Gap identified:** Competitors rely on vague, qualitative language; Active Oahu can inject exact Stat Density
- **15 fan-out queries** identified that competitors fail to answer comprehensively
- **Lexical homogeneity analysis** reveals overused generic phrasing across competitor sites

## Deep Research Prompts (3)

1. **Competitive Entity Mapping & IG Analysis** — Map competitive landscape, identify Consensus Baseline, 15 fan-out queries, quantitative data voids
2. **AEO Quick Answer Block Generator** — Generate 50-75 word extractable answer blocks for every page with statutory compliance
3. **Statistical Density Research** — Compile 15 verifiable stats from authoritative sources (HCRI, NOAA, Honolulu Ocean Safety)
