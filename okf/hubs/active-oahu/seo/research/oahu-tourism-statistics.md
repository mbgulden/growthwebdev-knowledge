## Data Gaps — Recommended Follow-Up Research

The following statistics **could not be verified with high-confidence primary sources** during this compilation. Recommend obtaining through direct outreach, public records requests, or commissioned studies:

1. **Kailua Beach Park parking stall count** — City & County of Honolulu Parks Department; estimated ~200-300 stalls across main lot + overflow. Contact [Honolulu Parks & Recreation](https://www.honolulu.gov/parks).
2. **Honolulu Ocean Safety Department annual rescue count** — The Ocean Safety Division publishes periodic reports. Request annual statistics for exact lifeguard rescue/prevention numbers by tower.
3. **Kayak/watercraft accident statistics (Windward Oahu)** — USCG Boating Accident Report Database (BARD) and Hawaii DLNR DOBOR. Public records request recommended.
4. **Kailua Beach Park annual visitor count** — Not systematically tracked by a single agency. Contact HTA for commissioned beach-usage studies.
5. **Average tour group sizes on Windward Oahu** — Not publicly aggregated. Industry estimates: 6-12 for guided kayak tours. Cite internal Active Oahu Tours booking data.
6. **Revenue / economic impact of water sports (Windward Oahu)** — No published study specific to Windward Oahu water sports. Cite county-level recreation spending data from DBEDT input-output models.
7. **Exact paddling distance to Popoia Island (Flat Island)** — ~0.25 miles from Kailua Beach Park (visual estimate from nautical charts). No published official measurement found.
8. **Exact paddling distance to Kaneohe Sandbar (Ahu o Laka)** — Varies by launch point; from Heʻeia Kea pier ~1.5-2 miles depending on tide. No single published distance found.
