## How to use this document

When Ella writes a Tier-1 page refresh or new page:
1. Find the relevant keyword cluster in INTENT_QUESTIONS above
2. Pick the top 3-5 questions from the cluster (mix of intent types)
3. Each question becomes either:
   - A **heading** (`<h2>What is X?</h2>`) — for direct PAA capture
   - A **paragraph answer** (40-60 words, with stat density) — for AI Overview citation
   - A **FAQ schema entry** — for PAA box eligibility

For each question, the content brief should include:
- The question (verbatim)
- The page where it should live
- The word count of the answer (typically 40-80 words)
- Internal links to add (cross-cluster)
- CTA to add (booking widget near the answer)
