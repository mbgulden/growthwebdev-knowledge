## The 5 strategic pillars

### Pillar 1: Close the Lanikai + Kailua + Waimanalo content gap

**Why:** KBA gets 111,934 visits/mo on their Lanikai guide. AOT gets 37. That's 3,000x gap.

**Action:**
- Build 4,000-word Lanikai Beach Guide pillar (NEW page)
- Strengthen existing Kailua Beach Park Guide to 3,000 words
- BRIEF-001 Waimanalo Guide (3,500 words, exists as brief)
- Internal linking hub-and-spoke from each pillar

**Target:** Even 5% capture of KBA's traffic = **+7,000 visits/mo potential**

### Pillar 2: Push striking-distance keywords to page 1

**Why:** GSC shows ~30 keywords in positions 4-15 with 100+ impressions each. Moving these to top 3 = +500-1,000 clicks/mo.

**Top targets (from GSC real data):**

| Query | Impr | Pos | Potential if moved to #3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| sharks cove oahu | 4,800 | 4.2 | +50-100 clicks/mo |
| electric beach | 5,935 | 6.5 | +50-100 clicks/mo |
| sharks cove | 4,987 | 5.9 | +50-100 clicks/mo |
| kailua beach park | 2,853 | 9.7 | +50 clicks/mo |
| kayak rental oahu | 291 | 13.1 | +20 clicks/mo |

**Action:** Schema + FAQ + AEO block + photo refresh on each target page.

### Pillar 3: Build "near me" + voice-search pages

**Why:** GSC shows 153 queries with "near me" modifier. AOT has zero `/near-me/` pages.

**Action:**
- `/near-me/kayak-rental-near-me/` (new)
- `/near-me/snorkel-rental-near-me/` (new)
- `/near-me/paddleboard-rental-near-me/` (new)
- `/near-me/e-bike-rental-near-me/` (new)
- LocalBusiness schema + geo-coordinates + opening hours
- "Open now" badges (using FareHarbor real-time data)

**Target:** Capture 20% of "near me" traffic in our geographic market = +200 visits/mo

### Pillar 4: Expand Japanese market

**Why:** GSC confirms Japan traffic exists (48 clicks from `/ja/activities/sharks-cove-self-guided-snorkel/`). Japan CTR 2.6% vs USA 0.5%. Massive opportunity.

**Action:**
- Build Japanese versions of top 5 revenue pages
- Add Japanese FAQ schema
- Submit Japanese pages to Japanese travel sites
- Partner with Japanese travel agencies (separate workstream)

**Target:** 200 Japanese clicks/mo by Q4

### Pillar 5: AI Overview capture

**Why:** AI Overviews now appear for most Oahu tourism queries. Being CITED in the AI Overview = new top position.

**Action:**
- For each top 30 query, add:
  - 40-80 word direct-answer paragraph (above the fold)
  - FAQPage schema with 4-6 Q&As
  - HowTo schema where applicable
  - E-E-A-T signals (author bio, last-updated date, citations)

**Target:** 8+ AI Overview citations by Q3
