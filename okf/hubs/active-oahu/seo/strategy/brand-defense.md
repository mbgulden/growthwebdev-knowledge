# Brand Defense Strategy — Protecting High-CTR Brand Queries

## Why brand defense matters

AOT's brand queries generate **25%+ of all clicks** at **47% CTR**:

| Query | Position | Clicks | CTR |
|---|:---:|---:|---:|
| `active oahu tours` | 1 | 150 (90d) | 47.0% |
| `active oahu` | 2 | 110 (90d) | 36.9% |

**Losing these positions would be catastrophic.** A drop from #1 to #3 could cost 100+ clicks/month — equivalent to losing a Tier-1 ranking for `sharks cove snorkeling`.

## Why brand positions can drop

1. **Competitor creates similar brand** (e.g., "Active Oahu Tours Hawaii") — trademark issue
2. **Domain change** — losing `activeoahutours.com` would lose everything
3. **Site redesign** breaking structured data
4. **Manual action** from Google for spam
5. **Brand confusion** — competitor bids on brand keywords in paid search

## Defense checklist

### Domain & technical

- [ ] **Domain registered long-term** — Verify `activeoahutours.com` registered through 2030+
- [ ] **SSL cert auto-renewing** — Cloudflare handles this; verify
- [ ] **No manual actions in GSC** — check monthly
- [ ] **No security issues** — Cloudflare protection
- [ ] **All subdomains redirect to canonical** — `www.` → root or vice versa, consistently

### On-page brand signals

- [ ] **Title tag uses brand name** — every page should have "Active Oahu Tours" in title
- [ ] **H1 mentions brand** — at least homepage and major pages
- [ ] **Footer brand block** — contact info, address, phone, social profiles
- [ ] **About page** — clear brand story, founders, history
- [ ] **Contact page** — multiple contact methods, real address (134B Hamakua Dr.)

### Off-page brand signals

- [ ] **Social profiles** consistent (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Tripadvisor, Yelp)
- [ ] **Knowledge Graph entry** — claim if possible
- [ ] **Wikidata entry** — for "Active Oahu Tours" entity
- [ ] **Press mentions** — Honolulu Magazine, Hawaii News Now, etc.
- [ ] **Industry directories** — Yelp, TripAdvisor, Hawaii Tourism Authority

### Paid search defense

- [ ] **Bid on own brand keywords** — even if organic #1, paid #1 prevents competitor hijacking
- [ ] **Use exact match** for "active oahu tours" and variants
- [ ] **Daily budget: $20-30** is enough to dominate brand SERP

### Negative SEO defense

- [ ] **Monitor backlinks** — alert if 100+ spammy links appear in a week
- [ ] **Disavow file** — keep updated
- [ ] **Brand mention monitoring** — Google Alerts for "Active Oahu Tours"

## Review generation strategy

Brand queries with high CTR often pair with **review intent**. Make sure:

- **TripAdvisor reviews** — actively request from happy customers
- **Google Business Profile reviews** — at least 50+ 5-star reviews
- **Yelp reviews** — at least 20+ reviews
- **Facebook reviews** — at least 30+ 5-star ratings

**Process:**
- Email customers 3 days post-booking asking for review
- Provide direct links to review platforms
- AOT responds to ALL reviews within 48 hours

## Brand narrative consistency

Every touchpoint should reinforce:
- **Active Oahu Tours** = local operator (14 years, founded 2012)
- **134B Hamakua Dr., Kailua** = real storefront (not just online)
- **Founded by Kimo + team** (real people)
- **Specialties:** Kailua kayak, Sharks Cove snorkel, Lanikai, Mokulua Islands, e-bike

Any content that contradicts this narrative dilutes brand equity.

## Monitoring

### Weekly check (automated)

- Brand query rankings (position changes)
- Brand query CTR (drops = title/meta issue)
- Brand impressions (drops = indexing issue)

### Monthly check

- Backlink profile changes (new links from spammy sources?)
- Brand mentions (any negative press?)
- Competitor brand SERP (anyone else bidding on "active oahu tours"?)

### Quarterly check

- Domain registration renewal date
- SSL cert renewal date
- Knowledge Graph / Wikidata entries current

- [ ] **Site links** — Google may show 6 sub-pages below the main link
