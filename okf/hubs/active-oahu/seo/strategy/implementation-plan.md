# Implementation Plan: GRO-795 SEO Research & Content Strategy for 3-Location SEO Foundation

This plan outlines the approach to establish a dominant SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) foundation for Active Oahu Tours across three primary Windward Oahu locations: Kailua, Lanikai, and Waimanalo.

## Approach & Methodology
1. **Analyze Sourced Data**: Synthesize insights from the SEO report, competitor audits (Surf N Sea, Hawaii Beach Time, Kailua Beach Adventures), local tourism stats, and owner interview templates.
2. **Design the Topic Cluster**: Create a hub-and-spoke content model mapping Kailua, Lanikai, and Waimanalo with structured internal linking rules to pass topical authority.
3. **Develop GEO/AEO-First Content Briefs**: Every brief will feature:
   - Question-based H2/H3 headers
   - Structured AEO Quick Answer blocks (50-75 words, high-density facts, quote-ready)
   - Specific statistical integration using verified local records
   - Custom Schema configurations
4. **Draft Schema Templates**: Build syntactically valid JSON-LD schema models (FAQPage, HowTo, LocalBusiness, Product) tailored to the specific constraints of each location.
5. **Prioritization Framework**: Evaluate each candidate page on Impact vs. Effort, taking into account competitor strengths and AOT's operational model.
