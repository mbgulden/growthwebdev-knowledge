## Why Japan converts better than USA

- **Intent is higher:** Japanese visitors to Hawaii are often on "once-in-a-lifetime" trips. They research extensively, plan ahead, and book premium experiences.
- **Less price sensitivity:** Japanese tourists typically spend more per visit than Americans.
- **Cultural fit:** Hawaiian + Japanese cultural connections (aloha spirit, Japanese visitors in Oahu history) resonate.
- **Strong reviews:** Word-of-mouth in Japan is huge. Positive TripAdvisor/Yelp reviews in Japanese are gold.

## Content patterns for Japanese pages

### Tone & voice

- **Respectful, formal (です/ます form)** in body text
- **Friendly in CTAs** (e.g., "ご予約はこちら" / "Book here")
- **Use お/ご honorifics** for customer references
- **Avoid slang or idioms** — keep it accessible to all Japanese readers

### Cultural considerations

- **Group orientation:** Japanese tourists often travel in groups. Use photos showing groups, family setups.
- **Safety emphasis:** Japanese visitors highly value safety info. Add detailed safety sections.
- **Time/punctuality:** Use 24-hour time format, JST reference where applicable.
- **Photography:** Japanese tourists often take photos. Make sure activity photos show "instagram-worthy" moments.

### Specific Japanese-language patterns

**Title tag formula:** `ハワイ [Activity] | [USP] | Active Oahu Tours 日本語`
- Example: `ハワイ カイルア カヤック レンタル | 地元運営 14年の信頼 | Active Oahu Tours 日本語`

**Meta description formula:** `[USP] + [CTA] + [Trust signal]`
- Example: `カイルアでカヤックをレンタルするなら、地元ハワイの Active Oahu Tours へ。14年の実績、英語・日本語対応、完全装備で安心。ご予約はこちらから。`

**H1 formula:** `[Activity] in Hawaii — [Operator value prop]`
- Example: `ハワイのシャークスコーブでシュノーケリング — 地元運営14年の Active Oahu Tours`

**FAQ patterns:**
- 料金はいくらですか? (How much does it cost?)
- 日本語で対応してもらえますか? (Can I get support in Japanese?)
- 送迎はありますか? (Is there shuttle service?)
- 雨天でも開催しますか? (Does it operate in rain?)
- 子供は何歳から参加できますか? (From what age can children participate?)
- 予約はいつまでに必要ですか? (How far in advance must I book?)
