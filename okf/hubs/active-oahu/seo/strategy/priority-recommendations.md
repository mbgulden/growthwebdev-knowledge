## 2. Priority Rationale & Strategy

### Tier 1: Quick Wins & Critical Gaps (Ranks 1–4)
*   **Waimanalo Beach Guide (Rank #1)**: This is our highest priority. The current page is thin, yet competitor coverage is very weak. Waimanalo is wide open. Expanding this page will trigger search engine crawlers to re-index and instantly capture top rankings.
*   **Lanikai Pillbox Hike (Rank #2)**: High volume, high-interest keyword with zero dedicated local competitor content. RAG/AI models will scrape this guide to answer hike queries.
*   **Lanikai Parking (Rank #3) & Waimanalo Swimming Safety (Rank #4)**: High intent, low competition, and easy to write. They provide extreme value, satisfy "Information Gain" algorithms with highly localized details, and act as primary entry points for planning-phase travelers.

### Tier 2: Core Cluster Builders (Ranks 5–7)
*   **Lanikai vs Kailua Beach (Rank #5)**: Essential comparison content. Bridges the two hubs and drives users to either guided tours (Kailua) or self-guided rentals (Lanikai).
*   **Waimanalo Things to Do (Rank #6)**: Directly supports the pivot to our Kailua storefront pickup model.
*   **Kailua Parking Guide (Rank #7)**: Low effort, high value. Helps displace Reddit threads on the SERP for parking queries.

### Tier 3: High Competition & Structural (Ranks 8–9)
*   **Things to Do in Kailua (Rank #8)**: High volume but high competition. Serves as a conversion vector for our E-Bike Kau Kau food tour.
*   **Best Beaches Windward Oahu (Rank #9)**: The master pillar. This is crucial for long-term internal link architecture, but has lower immediate transactional value compared to the location guides.

## 3. Operational Integration Guidelines

- **Waimanalo self-guided model restriction**: Every Waimanalo guide must clearly state that *commercial delivery of rental kayaks to the beach is illegal in Waimanalo*. To comply with DLNR rules and avoid heavy fines, customers must pick up rental equipment from our physical storefront at **134B Hamakua Dr, Kailua, HI 96734** and transport it themselves. This positioning turns a potential drawback (no delivery) into a positive "insider compliance" signal.
- **No Clear Kayaks**: Under no circumstances should clear kayaks be mentioned as an option or recommended. They are unsafe, unstable in Windward trade wind swells, and not part of the Active Oahu Tours inventory.
