# Master Report: Windward Oahu 3-Location SEO Foundation Strategy

## Executive Summary
This document compiles the master strategy and registry of deliverables produced for **GRO-795: AGY: Research & Content Strategy for 3-Location SEO Foundation**.

The goal of this campaign is to establish dominant visibility in search engines and generative answer engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) for Active Oahu Tours across Kailua, Lanikai, and Waimanalo.

## 2. Priority Content Recommendations

1.  **Waimanalo Beach Guide (Rank #1)**: Expand existing page immediately. Wide open keywords with low competitor coverage.
2.  **Lanikai Pillbox Hike Guide (Rank #2)**: High-intent keyword with zero dedicated local competitor pages.
3.  **Lanikai Parking Guide (Rank #3)**: Essential local prep info with high search traffic.
4.  **Waimanalo Swimming & Safety (Rank #4)**: Solves direct customer safety queries with high authority.
5.  **Lanikai vs. Kailua Beach (Rank #5)**: Compares the two main locations to route bookings.
6.  **Waimanalo Things to Do (Rank #6)**: Captures local activity interest.
7.  **Kailua Parking Guide (Rank #7)**: Displaces Reddit threads on the SERP.
8.  **Things to Do in Kailua (Rank #8)**: High volume, high competition, routes to E-Bike tours.
9.  **Best Beaches Windward Oahu (Rank #9)**: The master pillar page connecting all hubs.
