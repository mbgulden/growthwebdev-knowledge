# Heart-Wall Clearing Trial

An empirical survey assessing the impact of identifying and releasing trapped emotions comprising the "Heart-Wall."

## Study Design and Profile
- **Subjects**: 158 self-reported participants carrying a diagnosed energetic "Heart-Wall."
- **Intervention**: Emotion Code sessions targeting emotions stored around the physical/energetic heart (mapped to G Center/Heart Center mechanics).
- **Outcome metrics**: Self-reported emotional health, feelings of connection, and relief from chronic chest tension.
- **Sponsor**: Commissioned by the Discover Healing organization.
- **Publication**: Published in a non-indexed journal.

## Outcomes and Results
- **Reported findings**: A high percentage of participants reported a subjective release of emotional heaviness and improved relationship dynamics post-intervention.
- **Replication status**: Lacks independent replication or peer-reviewed publication in indexed scientific medical databases (PubMed count is zero).

## Critical and Ethical Assessment
- **Subjective Survey bias**: Self-reported metrics are highly vulnerable to confirmation bias and participant expectation bias.
- **No Scientific Control**: The study did not control for therapeutic attention or positive expectation.
- **Ethical Rule**: While the qualitative reports are useful for understanding subjective outcomes, they do not establish objective scientific efficacy. This distinction must be maintained under the [Ethical Guardrails and Harm Reduction Framework](../ethical_guardrails_and_harm_reduction_framework.md) to preserve client autonomy and prevent false health claims.
