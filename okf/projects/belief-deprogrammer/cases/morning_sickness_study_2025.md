# Morning Sickness Study (2025)

A preliminary, self-reported trial investigating the impact of the Emotion Code on morning sickness symptoms in pregnant individuals.

## Study Design and Profile
- **Subjects**: 57 enrolled pregnant individuals experiencing morning sickness.
- **Completion rate**: Only 13 of 57 participants completed the self-reported logs (a 77% drop-out rate).
- **Intervention**: Self-guided or practitioner-guided Emotion Code clearing sessions targeting trapped emotions associated with nausea.
- **Sponsor**: Commissioned by the Discover Healing organization.

## Outcomes and Results
- **Reported findings**: The 13 participants who completed the trial reported a significant subjective reduction in nausea severity and frequency.
- **Replication status**: No peer-reviewed publications or indexed citations.

## Critical and Ethical Assessment
- **High Attrition Rate**: The massive drop-out rate (77%) introduces severe selection bias, invalidating the statistical relevance of the reported improvements.
- **No Control Group**: Without a control group (such as a sham magnet treatment group), it is impossible to separate the intervention's effect from the natural resolution of symptoms over time or the placebo effect.
- **Ethical Rule**: This trial cannot be presented as scientific evidence of clinical effectiveness. Under the [Ethical Guardrails and Harm Reduction Framework](../ethical_guardrails_and_harm_reduction_framework.md), practitioners must explicitly disclose these limitations to ensure informed consent.
