# Mouse Trauma Study (2025)

An empirical trial commissioned to study the physiological and behavioral effects of the Emotion Code compared to standard drug treatments.

## Study Design and Profile
- **Subjects**: 48 laboratory mice subjected to fear-conditioning protocols.
- **Intervention groups**: Control, drug-treated (standard pharmaceutical), and Emotion Code clearing.
- **Outcome metrics**: Freezing behavior duration, cortisol levels, and survival/recovery metrics.
- **Sponsor**: Commissioned by the Discover Healing organization.

## Outcomes and Results
- **Reported findings**: The study claimed that mice receiving Emotion Code clearing showed a statistically significant reduction in fear-induced freezing behaviors and lower cortisol levels, comparable to or exceeding the drug-treated group.
- **Replication status**: This trial lacks independent, peer-reviewed replication in mainstream indexed scientific journals.

## Critical and Ethical Assessment
- **Methodological Limits**: The trial was conducted under the auspices of the primary developer of the modality (Discover Healing), presenting a severe risk of funding bias.
- **Application to Humans**: Extrapolating behavioral metrics from fear-conditioned rodents to complex human cognitive and belief structures is scientifically invalid without rigorous clinical human trials.
- **Ethical Rule**: Interveners must never cite this study as clinical proof of efficacy for human deprogramming. This constraint conforms to the [Ethical Guardrails and Harm Reduction Framework](../ethical_guardrails_and_harm_reduction_framework.md).
