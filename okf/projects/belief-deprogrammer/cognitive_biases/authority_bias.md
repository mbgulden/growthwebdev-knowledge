# Authority Bias

Authority Bias is a major mechanism in both the initial acquisition of dogmatic beliefs and the subsequent resistance to logical challenge.

## Operational Definition
A subject accepts claims without critical evaluation simply because they are made by an recognized authority figure, guru, or group leader.

## Psychological Origins
Evolutionary adaptation to trust parental/tribal instruction for survival, and societal conditioning toward hierarchy.

## Mitigation Vector
This bias is mitigated by:
- [Socratic Questioning](../methodologies/socratic_questioning.md) (specifically deconstructing authority-based compliance).
- [Street Epistemology](../methodologies/street_epistemology.md) (by examining the reliability of the authority as an epistemic method).

## Safety and Ethics
Interveners must avoid setting themselves up as a counter-authority, as this merely replaces one authority bias with another. Autonomy-first protocols are defined in the [Ethical Guardrails and Harm Reduction Framework](../ethical_guardrails_and_harm_reduction_framework.md).
