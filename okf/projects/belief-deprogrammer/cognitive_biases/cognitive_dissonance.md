# Cognitive Dissonance

Cognitive Dissonance is the primary state of discomfort that triggers defense mechanisms (such as the Backfire Effect) or allows for belief change when resolved constructively.

## Operational Definition
The psychological stress felt when a subject is confronted with information that conflicts with their existing beliefs, leading to rationalization, avoidance, or belief revision.

## Psychological Origins
The human drive for internal consistency. When consistency is broken, the nervous system enters a state of alarm.

## Mitigation Vector
This state must be managed with extreme care. It is handled by:
- [Cognitive Behavioral Restructuring](../methodologies/cbt_restructuring.md) (which helps map and resolve contradictions).
- [Socratic Questioning](../methodologies/socratic_questioning.md) (which introduces dissonance in small, tolerable increments).

## Safety and Ethics
If cognitive dissonance triggers severe emotional or somatic distress, interventions must be paused. The protocols for managing this distress are outlined in the [Ethical Guardrails and Harm Reduction Framework](../ethical_guardrails_and_harm_reduction_framework.md).
