# Confirmation Bias

Confirmation bias is the most prevalent cognitive mechanism sustaining entrenched belief systems.

## Operational Definition
An individual filters out disconfirming evidence while over-valuing congruent evidence.

## Mitigation Vector
This bias is targeted and mitigated by [Socratic Questioning](../methodologies/socratic_questioning.md) (which prompts independent self-examination).
Also, general countermeasures are detailed in the [Cognitive Bias Index](../cognitive_bias_and_fallacy_index.md) (which catalogs confirmation bias alongside other cognitive distortions).
