# Ethical Guardrails & Harm Reduction Framework

This framework serves as the immutable ethical constitution for the Belief Deprogrammer project, establishing the absolute boundaries of cognitive intervention.

> [!CAUTION]
> Under no circumstances should the system or its practitioners engage in coercive persuasion, deception, or manipulative counter-indoctrination. Preservation of the subject's autonomy is paramount.

---

## 1. Ethical Boundaries of Intervention

### A. Deprogramming vs. Counter-Indoctrination
*   **Ethical Deprogramming**: Focusing on improving the subject's cognitive flexibility, critical reasoning, and epistemological tools. The goal is to empower the subject to evaluate their beliefs independently.
*   **Coercive Counter-Indoctrination**: Forcing a predetermined set of beliefs onto the subject, utilizing emotional manipulation, isolation, or shame. This is classified as a severe ethical violation.

### B. Consent and Cognitive Autonomy
All interventions must respect the subject's right to exit the dialogue. The practitioner must never bypass or override a subject's explicit refusal to engage. Replacement beliefs, particularly when using scripting modalities like [Remove-Replace Scripting](./methodologies/remove_replace_scripting.md), must be self-selected or aligned with the subject's design, rather than external dogmas.

### C. Transparency and Pseudoscience Disclosures
Certain clearing and diagnostic modalities—specifically [Applied Kinesiology (Muscle Testing)](./methodologies/muscle_testing.md)—are classified as pseudoscience by mainstream scientific organizations.
*   **Mandatory Disclosure**: Practitioners must explicitly inform subjects that muscle testing and energy-clearing protocols (such as [The Emotion Code](./methodologies/emotion_code.md) or [The Body Code](./methodologies/body_code.md)) lack independent, peer-reviewed scientific replication and must not be used as a substitute for professional medical, psychiatric, or psychological diagnosis and treatment.
*   **Boundary Enforcement**: Under no circumstances should an energy-clearing diagnosis override clinical evidence or advice.

## 2. Harm Reduction Protocols

### A. Managing Cognitive Dissonance & Dissonance Fatigue
Challenging core beliefs causes significant emotional distress. Practitioners must monitor the speed and intensity of dissonance:
*   *Titration Protocol*: If the subject exhibits severe physiological or emotional distress, the session must be immediately paused. Do not try to force a breakthrough.
*   *Action*: Re-establish psychological safety by validating the subject's feelings of vulnerability without validating the false premise. Pace the deprogramming sessions to avoid cognitive fatigue.

### B. Preventing Persecution Complexes
Direct attacks on a belief often confirm the group's "persecution narrative" (e.g. "they will try to change your mind").
*   *Protocol*: Avoid confrontational debates or labeling the group as a "cult" or "scam" directly.
*   *Action*: Focus questions on general epistemological principles (using [Street Epistemology](./methodologies/street_epistemology.md)) rather than the specific group's rules or personalities.
