# The Body Code

The Body Code is the second level of Dr. Bradley Nelson's system, expanding beyond trapped emotions to identify and correct physical and energetic imbalances across six primary categories.

## Operational Protocol
1.  **Select Category**: Use [Muscle Testing](./muscle_testing.md) to navigate through the Body Code mind maps:
    - **Energies**: Post-traumatic stress, allergy/intolerance, emotional residue, mental thoughts.
    - **Circuits and Systems**: Disconnected organs, glands, meridians, or energy bodies.
    - **Toxicity**: Heavy metals, chemical exposure, radiation, electromagnetic stressors.
    - **Pathogens**: Energetic signatures of viruses, bacteria, fungi, or mold.
    - **Structural**: Misalignments in bones, muscles, nerves, or connective tissues.
    - **Nutritional / Lifestyle**: Nutrient deficiencies, dehydration, sleep, or color imbalances.
2.  **Determine Specific Imbalance**: Query details of the specific imbalance found.
3.  **Release/Correct**: Roll a magnet over the Governing Meridian (usually three times) or apply specific physical corrections.
4.  **Verify**: Use [Muscle Testing](./muscle_testing.md) to confirm resolution.

## Human Design Centers Integration
Imbalance categories map directly to the Human Design centers to isolate bodily stored conditioning:
- **Defined/Undefined Spleen/Ajna**: Highly vulnerable to Toxicities and Pathogenic signatures.
- **Defined/Undefined Root/G Center**: Commonly manifests Structural misalignments and Energy blockages.
- **Defined/Undefined Sacral/Core**: Expresses Nutritional and Lifestyle imbalances.
