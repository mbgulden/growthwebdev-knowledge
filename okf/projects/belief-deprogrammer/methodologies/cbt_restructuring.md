# Cognitive Behavioral Restructuring (CBT-derived)

Cognitive Behavioral Restructuring is a systematic, structured methodology designed to identify and replace maladaptive thought patterns.

## Operational Protocol
1.  **Psychoeducation**: Explain the connection between thought patterns, emotional responses, and underlying beliefs (the cognitive model).
2.  **Identify Distortions**: Help the subject notice and catalog patterns like catastrophizing, black-and-white thinking, emotional reasoning, and personalization.
3.  **Thought Recording**: Document the automatic thoughts associated with the core belief as they arise.
4.  **Evidence Examination**: Create a two-column list separating objective facts supporting the belief from subjective assumptions or biases.
5.  **Alternative Explanation**: Draft alternative, rational, and evidence-based explanations for the same set of facts.

## Ethical Guardrails
We treat cognitive distortions as natural human survival adaptations, not intelligence flaws. To prevent cognitive dissonance fatigue (a high-risk state), sessions must be paced, focusing on one distortion at a time and maintaining active nervous system grounding checks, as detailed in the [Ethical Guardrails and Harm Reduction Framework](../ethical_guardrails_and_harm_reduction_framework.md).
