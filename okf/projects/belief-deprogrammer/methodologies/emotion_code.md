# The Emotion Code

The Emotion Code is an energy-clearing modality created by Dr. Bradley Nelson that seeks to identify and release "trapped emotions" from the body.

## Operational Protocol
1.  **Identify Trapped Emotion**: Use [Muscle Testing](./muscle_testing.md) and the Emotion Code Chart (a grid of 60 emotions divided into 6 rows and 2 columns) to pinpoint the specific trapped energy.
2.  **Determine Details (Optional)**: Query when the emotion was trapped, who absorbed it from, or if it is inherited.
3.  **Release the Emotion**: Roll a magnet over the Governing Meridian (from the forehead, over the head, to the base of the neck) three times (or ten times for inherited emotions) to release the energy.
4.  **Confirm Release**: Use [Muscle Testing](./muscle_testing.md) to verify that the emotion is cleared.

## Mapping to Human Design Centers
We map Nelson's 60 emotions directly to the Human Design centers to target the specific energetic locations of conditioning:
- **G Center**: Abandonment, Betrayal, Rejection, Love Unreceived, Heartache.
- **Solar Plexus**: Grief, Sadness, Sorrow, Despair, Depression.
- **Heart/Ego**: Guilt, Shame, Unworthy, Worthless, Humiliation, Pride.
- **Spleen**: Terror, Panic, Dread, Horror, Shock, Vulnerability.
- **Root**: Overwhelm, Helplessness, Lack of Control, Frustration.
- **Ajna**: Confusion, Indecisiveness, Creative Insecurity, Discouragement.
- **Head**: Anxiety, Worry, Nervousness.
