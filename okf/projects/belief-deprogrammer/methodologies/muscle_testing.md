# Applied Kinesiology (Muscle Testing)

Applied Kinesiology (Muscle Testing) is used as the primary diagnostic interface in the Emotion Code, Body Code, and Belief Code systems to query the subconscious mind.

## Operational Protocol
1.  **Establish Baseline**: Verify a clear "yes" and "no" response using a muscle testing method (e.g. sway test, ring-in-ring test, or arm-pull test).
2.  **Formulate Binary Queries**: Ask clear, closed, yes/no questions to locate imbalances or beliefs (e.g. "Is there a trapped emotion in Column A?").
3.  **Execute Test**: Observe the muscle resistance or body sway response to determine the answer.
4.  **Confirm Resolution**: Perform a post-clearing query (e.g. "Is this emotion/belief 100% cleared?") to verify effectiveness.

## Scientific and Ethical Assessment
Major medical and scientific organizations (such as the American Cancer Society and the Australian Government Department of Health) classify Applied Kinesiology as **pseudoscience**. Studies show that muscle testing does not perform better than random guessing under double-blind conditions.
- **Pseudoscience Risk**: Relying on muscle testing for physical or psychological diagnoses is dangerous.
- **Ethics Rule**: Interveners must never present muscle testing as a scientifically validated diagnostic tool, nor use it to override standard medical or psychological advice. Full transparency and informed consent are required under the [Ethical Guardrails and Harm Reduction Framework](../ethical_guardrails_and_harm_reduction_framework.md).
