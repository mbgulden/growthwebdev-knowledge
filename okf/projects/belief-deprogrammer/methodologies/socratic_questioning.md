# Socratic Questioning

A foundational deprogramming vector for cognitive restructuring.

## Operational Protocol
1. Establish rapport and psychological safety.
2. Ask open-ended questions targeting the epistemic foundation of the belief.
3. Highlight internal contradictions without hostilely confronting the individual.

## Ethical Guardrails
We strictly respect the subject's epistemic autonomy. We do not replace their belief with a predefined dogma, but instead prompt independent critical thinking. If somatic distress triggers, immediately apply the titration protocols in the [Ethical Guardrails and Harm Reduction Framework](../ethical_guardrails_and_harm_reduction_framework.md) (which defines active intervention boundaries).
