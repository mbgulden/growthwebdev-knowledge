# Street Epistemology

Street Epistemology is a non-confrontational intervention protocol designed to bypass defensive cognitive filters.

## Operational Protocol
1.  **Build Rapport**: Establish a friendly, non-judgmental dialogue. Use active listening.
2.  **Identify Claim**: Help the subject isolate a specific, central belief.
3.  **Define Terms**: Clarify key vocabulary to prevent linguistic sliding.
4.  **Establish Confidence Scale**: Ask the subject to rate their confidence in the belief from 0% to 100%.
5.  **Examine Method**: Ask *how* they arrived at that confidence level (e.g. faith, personal experience, authority).
6.  **Apply Test Cases**: Pose hypothetical scenarios to evaluate the reliability of their method (e.g. "If someone else used the same method and arrived at a different conclusion, how would we resolve that?").
7.  **Re-evaluate Scale**: Ask if their confidence level has changed.

## Ethical Guardrails
We strictly respect the subject's epistemic autonomy. We do not replace their belief with a predefined dogma, but instead prompt independent critical thinking. In accordance with the [Ethical Guardrails and Harm Reduction Framework](../ethical_guardrails_and_harm_reduction_framework.md), if the subject shows signs of somatic or emotional distress, the conversation must be paused immediately.
