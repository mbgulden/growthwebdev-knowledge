# Human Design Transit Briefings

## When to use
Use this skill when asked to generate a daily/current Human Design transit briefing, especially for Becca or family-facing guidance. The brief must be grounded in computed transit data, not generic horoscope language.

## Core principles
- **Compute first, write second.** Always run the HD engine for current transit gates and the recipient natal chart before composing.
- **Translate mechanics into action.** The most important output is a concrete “what do I actually do today?” instruction.
- **Screenshot-ready beats exhaustive.** Keep the final brief short, visually clean, and emotionally usable.
- **No invented activations.** Only mention gates/channels/centers backed by actual engine output or known natal data.
- **Avoid horoscope fog.** Use HD mechanics as the backbone, but phrase guidance in grounded household/action language.

## Standard workflow
1. Resolve the HD engine import path robustly before importing. In cron contexts, do **not** blindly append `/work/...` to `$PRISMATIC_HOME`; `$PRISMATIC_HOME` may already point at `/home/ubuntu/work`. Prefer trying candidates like `/home/ubuntu/work/OpenHumanDesignMCP/hd-mcp-server/src`, `$PRISMATIC_HOME/OpenHumanDesignMCP/hd-mcp-server/src`, and `$PRISMATIC_HOME/work/OpenHumanDesignMCP/hd-mcp-server/src`, then use the first existing path. On the orchestrator profile specifically, the working tree lives at `/home/ubuntu/.hermes/profiles/orchestrator/home/work/`, so try `/home/ubuntu/.hermes/profiles/orchestrator/home/work/OpenHumanDesignMCP/hd-mcp-server/src` first when running under that profile (recorded Aug 8 2026). The Hermes-mirrored path is real and active; do not assume the absence of an HD engine based on the failure of one candidate. **Run the engine from `execute_code`, not `terminal(cd && python3 -c)` — the shell wrapper rejects `python3 -c` heredocs containing `&` or certain escaping patterns as "backgrounding."** `execute_code` imports the modules cleanly and prints JSON results in one round-trip.
   - **Each `execute_code` call is a fresh interpreter process (recorded Aug 12 2026).** Imports and module-level state do not persist between calls — defining `transits` in one call does not make it available in the next. Plan the workflow as a single monolith that runs compute + overlay + family lookup + analysis in one `execute_code` block, OR re-import + re-compute at the top of every subsequent call. Importing the engine in a second call is cheap (sub-second) but the *variables* are not. If the analysis is complex enough to need iteration, prefer a single monolithic `execute_code` over stepwise calls.
2. Initialize the ephemeris and calculate today’s Julian Day in the requested/local timezone.
3. Run `calculate_transit_positions(target_jd=jd)` and collect unique transit gates plus planet/gate/line/center details.
4. Calculate the recipient natal chart with `calculate_natal_chart(...)` using UTC birth time converted from local birth timezone.
5. For natal gate comparisons, read `chart["all_active_gates"]` (or the explicit `personality_gates` + `design_gates` fields). Do **not** call `cosmic_calculator.collect_gates(chart)` — in this engine that helper expects a Julian Day, not a chart dict.
6. Overlay transits on natal gates with `compute_transit_overlay(chart, target_jd=jd)`.
7. Identify:
   - transit gates intersecting natal gates,
   - transit-completed natal channels,
   - temporarily conditioned centers,
   - a small number of gates/channels that create the strongest “today” theme.
   - de-duplicated conditioned channels by normalized gate pair; the current engine may surface mirrored pairs like `6-59` and `59-6` separately, but the brief should treat them as one channel.
8. Detect “strong mechanic” signals that should drive the headline:
   - A transit planet landing directly on a natal gate that is part of the recipient's incarnation cross (personality sun/earth or design sun/earth) — this is the strongest single signal, aim the headline at it.
   - A fast-moving transit (Sun, Mercury, Moon) hitting a natal gate — explain it as a same-day / hours-specific flash, not a multi-day theme.
   - A slow-moving transit (Saturn, Pluto, True Node, Lilith) hitting a natal gate — frame as a longer arc and weave it into the “today” feel.
   - For a Projector: if the overlay shows ALL six undefined centers temporarily conditioned, that is itself the headline signature — the recipient is being offered borrowed definition everywhere. Lead with that. The same logic applies *partially* — when 4+ transit gates concentrate on a single *defined* center, that center's invitation pressure is cranked for the day. Use the strongest of (cross-gate hit, slow-mover-on-natal, all-six-undefined-conditioned, single-center flood, **cross-flood** — all four incarnation-cross gates hit at once) as the headline lever.
9. Compose a brief under the requested line limit, prioritizing concrete actions over explanation.

## V2 screenshot-ready format
Use this shape when the prompt asks for V2:

```text
**BOLD ALL-CAPS HEADLINE.**
1-2 sentence opening grounded in today’s mechanics.

━ TODAY’S VIBE ━
🌅 Morning: ...
🌤 Afternoon: ...
🌙 Tonight: ...

━ ONE THING ━
→ One specific action the person can actually do today.

━ FOR YOUR PEOPLE ━
💫 Person: one sentence/action.
...

━ SHARE-WORTHY LINE ━
“Short memorable sentence.”

— Signature
```

The "ONE THING" arrow must be a real, executable household action (text someone, write one sentence, give a kid two specific choices). It must NOT be reflective language like "be mindful" or "trust your authority" — those are the universal bad examples. The ONE THING is the brief's whole reason for existing; if it could be deleted without losing anything, the brief is soft.

- **Signature rule**: when the cron prompt identifies the agent as "Sage" (Becca's briefings) or "Sage/Fred", sign as **Sage**. When the prompt is for Michael or a generic Fred run, sign as **Fred**. The cron prompts are consistent about this — don't normalize to one name. The `Sage` voice is the in-character identity for Becca's briefs even when the umbrella skill lives under the Fred profile — do not collapse to `Fred` for Sage runs.
- **Support files**: `references/sage-becca-briefing-workflow.md` — Sage → Becca V2 loop, family snippet discipline (no invented natal overlays for kids), action-writing guardrails, and the Jul 31 2026 run recap showing how the headline + ONE THING were picked from conditioned channels and cross-gate hits.

If the prompt instead specifies the older Sage → Becca format, honor the user-provided format exactly. Do not combine both formats unless requested.

## Action-writing rules
Good actions are concrete and executable today:
- “Text Michael: ‘I can do X, not Y.’”
- “Write today’s yes/no/limit in one note before replying.”
- “Give William two choices, not an open-ended menu.”

Avoid vague actions:
- “Be mindful.”
- “Trust your authority.”
- “Lean into the energy.”

## Pitfalls
- Do not skip the missing-skill notice if a cron invocation explicitly says a skill was unavailable.
- If the requested narrow cron skills are missing, look for and use this umbrella skill as the governing fallback instead of treating the missing skill as a blocker.
- Do not silently fall back to generic HD descriptions when the engine path/module import fails; find the engine repo or report the blocker.
- Do not include stale transit gates from a previous run. Transit data is per-run only.
- Do not over-explain the calculation in the final user-facing brief; use the mechanics internally, then deliver the distilled result.
- If a prompt says V2 but also includes an older Version B block, prioritize the V2 screenshot-ready order while preserving any explicit critical constraints like line count, missing-skill notice, and concrete So What actions. The older template is sometimes embedded inline in the prompt body (not labeled "Version B") — same rule applies: V2 order wins, explicit constraints from the older block survive.
- **`calculate_natal_chart()` signature** (current engine, confirmed Jul 30 2026): takes positional/keyword `(name: str, birth_dt: datetime, lat: float, lon: float, timezone: str, ephe_path: Optional[str])`. `birth_dt` MUST be a UTC-aware `datetime` — convert from local with `tz.localize(local_dt).astimezone(pytz.utc)`. Older docs/skill revisions sometimes show `year/month/day/hour/minute` kwargs; those signatures do NOT exist in the current engine. Calling with those kwargs raises `TypeError: calculate_natal_chart() got an unexpected keyword argument 'year'`.
- **`compute_transit_overlay` lives in `transit_engine`, NOT `cosmic_calculator`.** Import as `from transit_engine import compute_transit_overlay`. The `cosmic_calculator` module exposes `calculate_natal_chart`, `collect_gates`, `compute_defined_centers`, `compute_profile`, `determine_authority`, `determine_type`, `compute_incarnation_cross` — no overlay function.
- **`compute_transit_overlay(chart, target_jd=jd)` return shape**: dict with keys `target_jd`, `target_datetime`, `transit_positions` (per-planet gate/line/center), `conditioning.conditioned_channels` (list of `{gates:[a,b], name, natal_has, transit_provides}`), `conditioning.conditioned_centers` (list), `conditioning.new_transit_gates` (list), `combined_defined_centers`, `combined_all_gates`, `interpretation_hints` (list of human-readable strings — usable verbatim as fallback language if needed).
- **Do not trust `calculate_natal_chart()` output alone if the engine version drifts.** Michael's natal can come back with profile `2/4`, definition `Single`, centers `['Heart/Ego','Spleen']` instead of the user-supplied `3/5`, `Split Definition`, `['G','Heart/Ego','Spleen','Throat']`. Becca's engine output (Jul 30 2026) returned `type: None` and `personality_sun_gate: None` but DID return `profile: 6/2`, `authority: Splenic`, `defined_centers: ['G','Heart/Ego','Spleen']`, and `incarnation_cross` with the correct gates `{personality_sun:26, personality_earth:45, design_sun:6, design_earth:36}`. Treat the user-supplied baseline as authoritative for motivation/authority/centers/channels/cross, and use the engine strictly for today's transit gates and conditioned-channel list. Cross-check that the engine's personality-sun gate matches the expected incarnation cross sun (Michael's = 26.2, Becca's = 26) before relying on it.
- For `compute_transit_conditioning`, do not call it with the chart's `all_active_gates` field without first asserting it is a `set`. If the engine ever returns a list, wrap it (`set(chart["all_active_gates"])`) or the function silently miscounts conditioned channels.
- `compute_transit_overlay` accepts a full natal chart dict (it pulls `all_active_gates` and `defined_centers` itself). Do not pre-strip or rename those fields when feeding it.
- **`calculate_transit_positions()` per-planet dict shape** (confirmed Jul 31 2026): keys are `longitude` (float, degrees), `gate` (int), `line` (int), `gate_name` (str), `center` (str), `retrograde` (bool). The key is `longitude`, not `position_in_gate` or `position`. Iterating every dict and printing `data.get('position_in_gate', data.get('position'))` raises `TypeError: unsupported format string passed to NoneType.__format__` because both fields are `None`. Use `data['longitude']` for raw coordinate, `data['gate']` and `data['line']` for activation.
- **Planet list from `calculate_transit_positions`** (Jul 31 2026): `Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, True Node, Mean Lilith, True Lilith, Earth, South Node`. The `Mean Lilith` vs `True Lilith` distinction matters for channel-completion analysis (a true Black Moon Lilith landing on a natal gate completes a different mechanical circuit than mean Lilith). 15 planets total — `len(transits) == 15`.
- **Headline dimension beyond conditioned channels**: when 4+ transit gates all hit a single *defined* center (e.g. Sun 33, Mars 12, Jupiter 31, Uranus 20 all on the Throat), that is itself the headline. Projector-specific signal: defined Throat getting activated by multiple planets at once = invitation pressure crank. Pair with the strongest single channel completion (e.g. 20-10 Awakening) for the *mechanism* and the multi-center flood for the *pressure*. Today's Jul 31 2026 brief did exactly this.
- **Incarnation-cross flood is the strongest same-day signal (recorded Aug 8 2026).** When ALL FOUR gates of the recipient's incarnation cross are simultaneously activated by transits — i.e. transit Sun + Earth + North Node + South Node each land on one of the four cross gates — that is the rarest and loudest "today" lever. The mechanics: Sun and Earth always occupy opposing I Ching hexagrams (one gate apart in the wheel), and North Node + South Node likewise oppose, so the four gates of a Right Angle Cross are *naturally* a set the Sun-Earth and Node-South-Node pairs can light up together. Detected for Michael's Cross of Rulership 4 on Aug 8 2026: Sun in 7, Earth in 13, N.Node in 30, S.Node in 29 — all four gates (7/13/29/30) hit at once. Lead the headline with the cross-flood; channel completions underneath (e.g. 29-46 Discovery, 30-41 Recognition) are *mechanism*, the cross-flood is *signature*. This dimension sits alongside the existing four (cross-gate single hit, slow-mover-on-natal, all-six-undefined-conditioned, single-center flood) — when a cross-flood exists, prefer it as the headline.
- **Near-flood (3 of 4 cross gates hit) is a strong-but-not-strongest signal (recorded Aug 12 2026).** When the Nodes cover two cross gates but Sun+Earth don't both land on the remaining pair — e.g. Moon 7.5 + N.Node 30.6 + S.Node 29.6 hit three of Michael's Rulership 4 gates while Sun sits in Gate 4 (one gate away from cross gate 13) — treat it as a *near-flood*, not a flood. Name it in the opening sentence ("three of your four cross gates are lit today") but do NOT lead with it as the headline if a stronger lever exists (e.g. Saturn on a defined Heart/Ego gate completing the Money Line). The synthesis: name the near-flood for *flavor*, lead with the channel completion / defined-center flood / cross-gate direct hit for *action*. The near-flood still affects the brief — it justifies the "identity cranks loud today" tone — but the headline has to pick the lever that produces the cleanest ONE THING.
- **Cross-gate hits are the strongest single-day signal.** When a transit lands on a gate in the recipient's incarnation cross (personality sun/earth or design sun/earth), name it in the headline or opening sentence. For Becca the cross gates are 26 / 45 / 6 / 36 — only mention a cross hit when the engine confirms it. Do not invent cross activations to flavor the brief.
- **Do not invent natal overlays for family members.** When the brief asks for a kid snippet and the kids' full natal data is not on file, write each snippet from the *collective* transits plus known family context, phrased as practical support. The umbrella skill is for the recipient's mechanics; family language is household support, not per-child HD exposition.
- **Blank lines count toward V2 line caps (Aug 5 2026 lesson).** When the cron prompt says "under N lines total," that N is a literal visual line count *including blank separator rows* between sections, not just non-blank lines. If the prompt says 18 and you draft with a blank row between every section (`━ TODAY'S VIBE ━`, `━ ONE THING ━`, etc.), you blow past the cap even with terse content. The fix: write each `━ SECTION ━` divider on the line *immediately* following the previous section's last content line — collapse to no separator rows. The dividers stay visible and screenshot-readable; you save 4–6 lines that way. If the prompt allows more than 18, blank rows are fine; if the cap is tight, compress.
- **ONE THING bracket-slot pattern for Projector boundary-setting actions (Aug 5 2026).** When the ONE THING is a "text Michael" / "write one note" action that requires the recipient to inject their own specifics, use `[bracketed slots]` rather than a fully filled-in sentence: e.g. `Text Michael: "Today I can own [one thing]; I need you to own [the other]."` followed by a closing instruction (`stop negotiating until your gut clicks`). This keeps the action concrete and executable while preserving the recipient's agency to fill in their real day. Stays screenshot-readable; avoids pretending to know details you don't.
